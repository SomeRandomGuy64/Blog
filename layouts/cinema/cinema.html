    When you’re free
    Google Maps API key — optional, for real public-transport times
    Without a key, journeys are straight-line estimates. With one, the planner asks Google for bus/tram/train (and short walking) times for just the journeys it needs, straight from this browser, and caches them here for 30 days. The key is stored only in this browser. It needs the Routes API enabled on its Google Cloud project and must not be restricted to other APIs or websites that exclude this page. Requests count against your Google billing / free monthly allowance. How to get a key
