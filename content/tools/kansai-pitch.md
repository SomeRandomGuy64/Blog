Type a word to see its **Kansai (Keihan) pitch accent**. It shows Kyoto by default, and you can
switch region to Osaka, Hyōgo or the Shikoku glossaries.

- **Attested** pitch comes from accent dictionaries: 16-speaker Kyoto surveys, recorded Kyoto
  conversations, Osaka rakugo, Omizan Sakamoto's Kinki accent dictionary and regional glossaries.
- **Verbs and adjectives** also get every Kyoto conjugation (書かへん, 書いた, 書きよる…), worked out
  from the Kyoto survey's sample words.
- **Nouns no dictionary has** get a best guess from their Tokyo accent and from words that share
  their kanji. About three in four guesses come out exactly right.
