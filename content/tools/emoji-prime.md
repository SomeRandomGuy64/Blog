[Emoji prime](https://github.com/SomeRandomGuy64/emojiPrime) puts pictures over a Japanese video as each
word is said: an emoji or an [Irasutoya](https://www.irasutoya.com/) illustration for each word, one or two
illustrations for each sentence, and an emoji "rebus" that carries the idea of the line. There's no English,
so you follow along with the pictures and keep listening.

This player plays a video file from your computer together with its `web.json` pack, which the
emoji-prime skill writes into a `<video name>.emojiprime/` folder next to the video. **Open a folder** (Chrome
or Edge) and it lists every video in it that has a pack, remembers the folder and where you stopped in each
video, and plays them one after another. Or **add a video** by picking the video and its pack yourself.

If you're logged in, the time a video actually plays (unmuted) is added to your [Track](/track/), under
Active immersion › Emoji Prime. Your videos stay on your computer: nothing is uploaded.
